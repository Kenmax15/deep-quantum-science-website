2. Hosting and server log files

This website runs on Cloudflare Pages (Cloudflare, Inc., 101 Townsend St, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA). When you visit the site, Cloudflare processes technically necessary data: IP address, date and time, the page requested, referrer and browser type. Cloudflare participates in the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework; transfers to the USA rely on the European Commission's adequacy decision (Art. 45 GDPR). Cloudflare also hides our email address from spam robots on the page. More: Cloudflare privacy policy.

All connections are encrypted (HTTPS/TLS).

3. Cookies, local storage and your consent

This website sets no cookies. In your browser's local storage it keeps only three small settings, which never leave your device: your language (EN/DE), your light/dark choice, and your answer to the consent question. These are strictly necessary for the functions you choose (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG) and need no consent. You can delete them at any time in your browser settings.

4. Audience measurement (only with your consent)

If you click “Accept statistics”, we count visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics. It sets no cookies, does not create a profile of you and does not follow you across websites. When the page loads, your browser sends Cloudflare anonymous technical data (page address, referrer, browser type, screen size, loading times); your IP address is used only to deliver the request and is not stored for analytics. We see only aggregated figures (for example, how many people visited a page). Legal basis: your consent, Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and § 25(1) TDDDG. If you choose “Only necessary”, nothing is loaded. You can change your choice at any time with Cookie settings (also at the bottom of every page); withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of earlier processing.

5. Contact form and email

If you use the contact form, we process your name, email address, optional organisation and your message to answer your request (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR for enquiries about our services, otherwise Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). The form sends your message by email to us through the email service Resend (Resend, Inc., USA; servers in the EU, Ireland), which acts as our processor; Resend is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. To block spam, a hidden field and the time spent on the form are checked, and the number of messages per visitor is limited; for this a one-way hash of your IP address is kept for one hour and then deleted automatically. We do not store your message on the website; we keep the email only as long as needed to handle your request, unless statutory retention periods apply.

If you email us directly, we process your details in the same way.

6. Links to social networks

The site contains plain links to LinkedIn, YouTube and Facebook. No plugins are embedded; data is transferred to the provider only when you click a link, and then the provider's own privacy policy applies.

7. Software download

The installer of DeepQ Manuscript is provided via GitHub (GitHub, Inc., USA). When you click the download button, your browser connects directly to GitHub, which processes your IP address and technical data under its own privacy statement. GitHub participates in the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

We receive your name, email address and the transaction details. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

Order records. For each order we store the name on the licence, your email address, the PayPal transaction number, the licence key and the time of your consent with our host Cloudflare.
